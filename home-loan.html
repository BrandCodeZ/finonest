FAQs

Home Loan — Frequently Asked Questions

What is the lowest home loan interest rate available?+

Home loan rates currently start at 7.30% p.a. for eligible salaried applicants. Your final rate depends on your credit score, income, loan amount and the lender you choose — we help you pick the lowest available offer.

How much home loan can I get?+

Most lenders offer up to ₹5 crore with Loan-to-Value (LTV) financing up to 90% of the property value. Your exact eligibility depends on your income, age, credit score and the property's valuation.

How quickly will my home loan be approved?+

With pre-approved offers and our digitized process, eligible applicants often get in-principle approval within 24 hours. Final disbursal usually completes within 5–10 working days.

Can I prepay or foreclose my home loan?+

Yes. Most lenders allow partial or full prepayment with zero charges on floating-rate home loans (as per RBI guidelines). Fixed-rate loans may carry a small prepayment fee.

Do you help with NRI home loans?+

Absolutely. We assist NRIs with home loans in India including for purchase, construction and resale of property, with specialised documentation support and doorstep coordination.
