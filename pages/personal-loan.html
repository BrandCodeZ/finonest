FAQs

Personal Loan — Frequently Asked Questions

How much personal loan can I get with a ₹25,000 salary?+

With a monthly take-home of ₹25,000 and a good credit profile, most lenders approve personal loans between ₹2–5 lakh. Higher loans need higher income and stronger credit history.

What is the minimum credit score required?+

A CIBIL score of 700 or above is preferred by most lenders. With 750+, you can get faster approval and lower interest rates.

Is collateral required for a personal loan?+

No. Personal loans are unsecured — no collateral or guarantor is needed. Approval depends on your credit score and repayment capacity.

How quickly will I receive the loan amount?+

For pre-approved offers with verified KYC, funds can be disbursed within 24 hours directly to your bank account.

Can I prepay my personal loan?+

Yes, most lenders allow full prepayment typically after 6–12 EMI payments, sometimes with a small foreclosure fee as per lender policy.
