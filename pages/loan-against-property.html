FAQs

Loan Against Property — Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a loan against a property that's still under a home loan?+

Yes. If you hold clear title and are current on your existing home loan, lenders may offer a top-up loan or refinance against the property's appreciated value.

Will I lose my property if I default?+

As with any secured loan, lenders have the right to enforce the property in case of extended default. Loan Against Property should be used with a clear repayment plan — our advisors help structure EMIs comfortably.

What is the minimum loan amount for LAP?+

Most lenders start LAP at ₹10–15 lakh, although higher amounts (₹50 lakh+) get the most competitive rates and flexible structures.

Can a rented property be mortgaged for a loan?+

Yes. Commercial or residential properties that are rented out can be mortgaged; rental income can actually strengthen your repayment capacity.

How long does LAP approval take?+

With clear title documents, in-principle approval is often available within 48 hours. Disbursal follows property valuation done by the lender, usually within 1–2 weeks.
