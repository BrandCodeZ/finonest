FAQs

Car Loan — Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get 100% financing on a car?+

Yes. For new cars, most of our partner lenders offer up to 100% of the on-road price including insurance, RTO charges and accessories as part of the financed amount.

What is the maximum car loan tenure?+

New car loans can be taken for up to 7 years. Used car loans generally allow up to 5 years depending on the age of the vehicle.

How fast can I get my car loan approved?+

With a good credit score and complete documents, you can get same-day in-principle approval. Disbursal typically happens within 2–3 working days.

Can I finance a second-hand car bought from an individual?+

Yes, we finance used cars purchased from dealers and individual sellers too. Documents like the RC transfer and vehicle valuation report are required.

Is there a part-payment option for car loans?+

Most lenders allow part-prepayment of car loans, often with minimal or no charges depending on the loan type and tenure.
