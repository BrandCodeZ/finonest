FAQs

Credit Card — Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide lifetime free credit cards?+

Yes. Several of our partner banks offer lifetime free cards — no joining fee, no annual fee, forever. We also help negotiate first-year waivers on premium cards.

What is the minimum credit score for a credit card?+

A CIBIL score of 750+ gives you access to premium cards. Customers with scores between 650–750 can still get entry-level and secured cards.

How soon will I receive my card?+

Once your application is approved, banks typically deliver the card within 5–7 working days. My Card 24×7 access starts as soon as approved.

Can I get a credit card with no salary proof?+

Secured credit cards (against a fixed deposit) can be issued without income proof. A few banks also issue cards based on strong bank balances or existing relationships.

Is a credit card application free?+

Yes — applying through Finonest is completely free. We compare offerings across banks and recommend the best card without any service charge to you.
