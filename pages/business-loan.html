Business Loan Eligibility & Documents

✅ Eligibility Criteria

  • Business Vintage: Minimum 1 year in operation
  • Turnover: Minimum ₹25 lakh per annum (varies by lender)
  • Age: 21 – 65 years (proprietor / directors)
  • Credit Score: 700+ preferred (business or personal)
  • ITR: Last 1–2 years income tax returns filed

📄 Documents Required

  • Identity & KYC: PAN, Aadhaar of all proprietors / directors
  • Business Proof: GST registration, incorporation certificate
  • Financials: Last 1–2 years ITR, P&L, balance sheet
  • Bank Statements: 6–12 months current account statements
  • Business Address: Rental agreement / ownership proof + utility bill
FAQs

Business Loan — Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a business loan without collateral?+

Yes. MSME loans, government-backed unsecured schemes and several NBFC products offer collateral-free business loans up to ₹75 lakh (and higher in some cases) based on turnover and credit profile.

What is the maximum tenure for a business loan?+

Unsecured business loans typically offer tenures of 12–60 months. Secured loans like commercial property or OD against property can go up to 15–20 years.

How fast can funds be disbursed?+

For eligible MSME applicants with GST returns and clean bank statements, disbursal can happen within 24–72 hours after document verification.

Is my business required to have filed GST for loan approval?+

Not always — it depends on the lender and product. GST-registered businesses usually get faster approvals and higher eligibility, but some lenders accept ITR-based assessment.

Can I get a loan for a newly started business?+

New businesses (under 1 year) can still apply for loans backed by collateral or a strong co-applicant profile. Some startup schemes also support fresh ventures with a good business plan.
