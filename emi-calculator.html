What is an EMI?

Equated Monthly Instalment — the fixed amount you pay every month towards repaying your loan.

💡

Two Components

Every EMI contains principal (the amount you borrowed) and interest (the lender's charge). Your EMI stays fixed for the whole tenure.

📉

Reducing Balance

Banks use the reducing-balance method — interest is charged only on the outstanding amount. So the interest share falls every month while the principal share rises.

🧮

The Formula

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ [(1 + r)n − 1], where P = principal, r = monthly interest rate, n = number of months.

FAQs

EMI Calculator — Frequently Asked Questions

What factors affect my EMI?+

Three things decide your EMI: the loan amount, the interest rate and the tenure. A larger loan or a lower tenure means a higher EMI; a longer tenure lowers the EMI but increases total interest.

Is this calculator accurate for my loan?+

The calculator uses the standard reducing-balance formula used by all Indian banks and NBFCs. Actual figures may vary slightly with the lender's exact pricing, processing fees and when the first EMI begins.

How can I reduce my monthly EMI?+

Choose a longer tenure, make a higher down payment to reduce the principal, or improve your credit score to qualify for a lower interest rate.

Should I prefer a shorter or longer tenure?+

A shorter tenure means higher EMIs but much lower total interest. A longer tenure gives lower EMIs but you pay considerably more interest over the life of the loan. Choose based on your monthly cash flow.

Does the processing fee affect my EMI?+

Processing fees are usually deducted from the sanctioned amount or paid upfront — they don't change the EMI but increase your total cost of borrowing. We show it separately so you see the full picture.
